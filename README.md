# cosmos3-canvas

[Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Auto](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Auto) as a [carla_canvas](https://github.com/hakuturu583/carla_canvas) renderer: the ego's RGB cameras and ray-cast LiDAR in CARLA are generated by the world model, closed loop, behind the CARLA API.

```yaml
renderer:
  backend: cosmos3_canvas:Cosmos3Renderer
  startup_timeout: 900
  timeout: 600
  options:
    checkpoint: /path/to/Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Auto
    cameras: [camera_front_wide_120fov]
    caption: "A daytime drive through a dry, sunlit city street with clear lane markings."
    horizon_seconds: 40
sensors:
  - sensor.lidar.ray_cast
  - sensor.camera.rgb
```

See [examples/cosmos3.yaml](examples/cosmos3.yaml) for a complete file, with the renderer started from its own environment.

## How it works

The model generates the Hyperion 8 cameras and the Pandar128 LiDAR together, one 0.4 s chunk at a time:

- 12 camera frames at 30 fps;
- 4 sweeps at 10 Hz;
- the first chunk is frame 0 and sweep 0.

| module | what it does |
|---|---|
| `stream.py` | Drives the framework's rolling sampler (`iter_rolling_transfer_ar`) one chunk at a time. The model, the plans, the text and the caption schedule are built by framework code from a two-chunk bootstrap request. The control and target latents are then latent buffers covering the whole rollout. Each chunk's controls are encoded with the VAEs' causal streaming encoders, and its outputs decoded with their streaming decoders. |
| `controls.py` | Draws the controls with the cookbook's renderers (`cosmos_auto`), one frame or sweep at a time: WSM v4 control frames, and LiDAR HD-map range maps. The map is the CARLA map's OpenDRIVE, exported to RDS-HQ with [roadgen](https://pypi.org/project/roadgen/). The poses and boxes come from the live scene. The renderers' steps that read the whole clip are replaced by ones that read only the past (the module's docstring lists them). |
| `renderer.py` | The carla_canvas renderer. It tracks the ego's sensors and the actors, and extrapolates their poses for the rest of a chunk (`trajectory.py`, from lidarverse_canvas). It generates a chunk when a tick needs one. |
| `sensors.py` | Turns the outputs into measurements. A CARLA camera samples its Hyperion camera's F-theta image along its pinhole rays; pixels outside the lens' field of view are black. A LiDAR ray reads the range-map cell it points at. |

Checked against the framework's batch inference on the same controls:

- The text tokens and plans are identical.
- The control latents differ by 0.3–0.8 % (camera, BF16 encode chunking) and 0 (LiDAR).
- The first frame agrees to 38 dB PSNR (`scripts/check_stream.py`, `scripts/diag_stream.py`).

## Install

The renderer needs:

- a [cosmos-framework](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-framework) checkout and its environment;
- the cookbook's control renderers (`.agents/skills/cookbooks/cosmos3/simulation/auto/wsm`, with Git LFS);
- `carla-canvas` and `roadgen`.

On GPUs FlashAttention-4 has no kernels for (sm120: RTX PRO 6000, RTX 50 series), the model needs [NVIDIA/cosmos-framework#302](https://github.com/NVIDIA/cosmos-framework/pull/302). Until it is merged, use the branch `fix/sm120-gapped-replay` of [hakuturu583/cosmos-framework](https://github.com/hakuturu583/cosmos-framework/tree/fix/sm120-gapped-replay).

carla-canvas needs protobuf ≥ 7.35. In the framework's environment that breaks wandb < 0.31, which the model imports. One way to set it up is a venv on top of the framework's:

```bash
uv venv -p cosmos-framework/.venv/bin/python canvas-venv
SP=canvas-venv/lib/python3.13/site-packages
echo "import site; site.addsitedir('$PWD/cosmos-framework/.venv/lib/python3.13/site-packages')" > $SP/zz_cosmos_framework.pth
printf '%s\n' $PWD/cosmos-framework/.agents/skills/cookbooks/cosmos3/simulation/auto/wsm $PWD/cosmos3-canvas > $SP/cosmos3_canvas_src.pth
uv pip install -p canvas-venv/bin/python carla-canvas==0.2.0 roadgen==0.37.1 'moderngl>=5.10' 'python-pycg>=0.7' mpire minio 'shapely>=2.1'
uv pip install -p canvas-venv/bin/python --no-deps 'wandb>=0.31'
# then remove from canvas-venv every package the framework's venv already has, except grpcio and protobuf
```

## Speed

On an RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q, one camera at 480p and the LiDAR, a 0.4 s chunk takes about 8.1 s, about 20x slower than real time:

| step | seconds |
|---|---|
| drawing the controls | 0.3 |
| encoding them | 0.47 |
| generating | 6.5 (4 denoising steps and the cache refresh) |
| decoding | 0.8 |

A 10 s scenario takes about 4 minutes. The generation is bound by the transformer's matrix multiplies. About 70 % of a chunk's tokens are the LiDAR's.

## Limits

- Traffic-light states are not sent by carla_canvas, so the lights are drawn "unknown" in the controls.
- The rollout's length is set up front (`horizon_seconds`). Past it, the sensors are dark.
- Every configured camera needs a CARLA camera on the ego, mapped by `camera_map` (role name to camera key) or by its yaw. The ego needs one ray-cast LiDAR; without one, a virtual LiDAR 1.94 m above the ego is used. Further LiDARs get no returns.
- Generated LiDAR is the Pandar128's: +15° to −25°, 5 to 100 m.
